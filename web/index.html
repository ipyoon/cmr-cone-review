Assign frames

Tick who reviews this task, or add someone new: the frames are split into even, back-to-back ranges, which you can adjust. Each reviewer first sorts the unknown cones in their frames, then reviews those frames. CVAT jobs that fall entirely in one person's frames are assigned to them in CVAT too.

New task

Upload the pictures or the video to review. The task is created in CVAT, the same as CVAT's own "Create a new task".

Export approved frames

Downloads a zip of every approved frame at full quality, with its boxes as YOLO labels: images/, labels/ and data.yaml. Frames whose boxes changed after approval are left out.

Or export from the terminal

Run in the project folder. It writes to verified_dataset/; for a video task add VIDEO_PATH=path/to/video first.
